Frames & iframes

Interacting with elements inside a frame requires switching context first. This page has a same-origin iframe, a nested iframe-in-an-iframe, and a frame that only loads after a delay.

Same-origin iframe stable

Switch into the frame's content document before interacting with its form.

Nested iframe (two levels deep)

An iframe inside an iframe — you need to descend twice to reach the innermost button.

Lazily-loaded frame timing-sensitive

No iframe exists in the DOM until you click the button — wait for the frame to attach, then wait for it to finish loading.
